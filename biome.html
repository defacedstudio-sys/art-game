Drop tileset .png / .tsx files anywhere to load real art, or a .tmx to add a map.

Order keeps biomes in clean regions; Chaos intermixes them. Relaxed→Fear sets the land: relaxed is beach, the middle is grass & trees, fear turns it purple. Imaginative floods the world with colour variants and pulls in dream tiles. Memory glitches the tiles and tears white void patches. Sloths roam the relaxed/chaotic side, houndmares the fearful/chaotic side. Zoom pulls back from 16 to 100 tiles across.
